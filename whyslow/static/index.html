How it works

Three quiet steps

No cloud, no account. Everything is measured and explained on this laptop.

01

Watches quietly

A small collector notes CPU, memory, disk and per-app usage every few seconds.

02

Spots slow moments

Each minute gets a slowness score based on how long programs were stuck waiting.

03

Finds the cause

It compares each app with its normal behaviour to name the one that tipped things over.
